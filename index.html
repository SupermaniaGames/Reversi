How to play

  • Two players, Black and White, take turns placing one disc on the 8 by 8 board. Black goes first.
  • A move must trap at least one enemy disc. Line up your new disc and another of your own discs with enemy discs between them, in a straight line.
  • Every trapped disc flips to your colour. Lines can run across, up and down, or on a diagonal, and one move can flip several lines at once.
  • Small dots on the board show where you can play. If you have no legal move, your turn is skipped automatically.

  • The game ends when neither player can move, or when all 64 squares are full.
  • Count the discs. The player with more discs of their colour wins. Equal counts are a draw.
  • The live count of each colour is shown on the player cards, and it changes as discs flip.
  • Corners can never be flipped, so they are the strongest squares. Edges are strong too.
  • Squares next to an empty corner are risky, because they can give the corner away.

  • Play with friends: one player creates a room and shares the 4-digit code or the WhatsApp link. You play a best of 3 series, and the first to win 2 games takes it. Draws are replayed.
  • Timer: online and against the computer you have 15 seconds per turn. If time runs out, a move is played for you. Miss 3 turns in a row and you lose the series online, or the game closes against the computer.
  • Computer: pick Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert. You choose who goes first.
  • Pass N Play: two people share one phone and take turns. There is no timer.
  • Legal move dots can be turned off when you set up a game.
  • Tip: take corners when you can, and avoid giving them away.
  • Tip: having fewer discs in the middle game is often better, because it leaves your opponent fewer moves.
